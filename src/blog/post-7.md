I spent this week improving how tags work so readers can find related topics faster.

## What I improved

I made three practical changes:

1. Standardized tag names to avoid duplicates.
2. Removed overly specific one-off tags.
3. Prioritized tags that describe real themes.

## Why this matters

A clean tagging system makes archive pages more useful and helps new readers discover older posts.

## Next target

I want to add related posts at the bottom of each article based on shared tags.
