I created a small publishing checklist and it reduced last-minute fixes.

## My current checklist

Before publishing, I verify:

1. Title and description are clear.
2. Date and tags are correct.
3. Links and formatting render as expected.
4. The closing section has a concrete takeaway.

## Why it works

A repeatable process saves energy and keeps quality consistent from post to post.
