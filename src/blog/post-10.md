I spent time on typography and spacing, and the difference is bigger than I expected.

## What I adjusted

I focused on readability improvements:

1. Better heading spacing.
2. More comfortable line length.
3. Clear separation between sections.

## Why this matters

Good typography helps readers stay focused and understand content faster.

## Next improvement

I want to create a consistent style for code blocks so technical posts look cleaner.
